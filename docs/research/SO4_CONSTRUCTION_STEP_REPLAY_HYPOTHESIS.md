# Optional construction-step replay: numerical isolation contract

Recorded before implementation. This is a separately versioned research
operator, not a correction applied retroactively to historical experiments.
Keep legacy graph-segment replay and native defaults unchanged.

## Mechanism and scope

Current controlled replay advances the heading kernel once per graph segment.
Daughter attachments subdivide those segments. Continuation keys contain graph
segment index, boundary keys contain node ID, and the initial frame retains
incidental legacy-heading roll. Adding graph cuts can therefore change a parent
trajectory even when its original construction curve and contour length agree.

Introduce `research_current_length_grid=None | "construction_steps_v1"`.
None retains the existing route. Initially qualify only controlled arm D,
Fichtl mode, max order 3, explicit matching seeds and keyed current-length CDFs
for orders 2/3. Native execution and the legacy replay entrypoint must reject
an active unsupported route rather than silently ignoring it. A named new
entrypoint selects this version explicitly.

The retained intervals are **construction steps** generated by the current
length sampler, including its random sorted weights. They are not measured
elongation events. Their use does not establish a biological time step,
step-size convergence or equivalence to the paper's CPlantBox discretization.
[Fichtl et al., 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11633832/).

## Partition and provenance

When active, preserve the actual original order-2/3 construction lengths and
boundary-node identities before attachment subdivision. Store sampler version,
stable organ identity, order, seed, original segment count and normalized CDF
identity. Add metadata only for the opt-in construction. Do not redraw lengths
or create a replacement partition to pass validation. At INITIAL export, read
positioned boundary geometry from the live nodes; do not retain stale local
coordinates through yaw, inclination or origin transforms.

Authenticate every construction boundary and its graph membership/order,
positive finite lengths, total contour length and sampler provenance. Require
the original corners to remain represented, using the existing absolute
1e-8 cm attachment/conservation tolerance. Reject missing, ambiguous or
inconsistent boundaries. No rescaling, inferred chords across missing corners
or tolerance relaxation.

## Integration then interpolation

For each organ, assign its Fichtl insertion direction using the existing
insertion and row-axial controls. Place the whole first construction step along
that direction subject to the common physical soil projection. Start heading
search at construction step two, with the unchanged kernel:

```text
alpha = sigma * sqrt(step_length_cm) * Z
candidate_count = 1 + stochastic_round(N * sqrt(step_length_cm))
```

Initialize with `frame(assigned)` and transport using accepted headings.
Freeze the basis-axis tie and antiparallel conventions. Version continuation
and boundary streams by route, seed, stable organ identity, construction-step
index and purpose; exclude graph node ID, graph rank and CDF hash from RNG
keys. CDF hashes identify inputs, not stochastic streams.

After direction selection and projection, interpolate graph attachment cuts
onto each accepted straight construction step. Interpolation consumes no RNG
and causes no additional search or projection. At a construction corner,
daughter insertion keeps the existing incoming-parent-tangent convention.
Keep topology, all stored edge lengths/dimensions, attachments and primary
scaffold unchanged within replay and validate with existing geometry checks.

Record diagnostics once per construction step, with an explicit mapping of
graph fragments to steps, so added cuts do not inflate intervention counts.
Expose route/version, recovered source partition, proposal innovations,
accepted headings and graph mapping. Retain node/edge/proposal exports with
clear step semantics.

## Synthetic qualification

Compare an invented parent with construction lengths [4,6] against graph cuts
[2,4,8,10]. Proposals, boundary decisions, endpoints and common arc-position
coordinates must agree. Repeat with surface crossing, pure-up fallback,
daughter attachment inside a step and exactly at a corner, and changed node IDs.
Test missing/ambiguous corners, wrong provenance, seed/config mismatch,
unsupported arms/native routing, finite rooted graphs and length conservation.
Keep default/None A-D and native regression outputs/RNG unchanged.

This isolates attachment subdivision only. Changing construction-step count
or weights changes the model. Changing branching topology can also renumber
later organs and shift shared construction/placement RNG. A subsequent
branching experiment must freeze or explicitly account for those effects;
this operator alone does not guarantee an identical regenerated scaffold.

Public-source design review: GPT-6 Astra/high and GPT-6.1 Sol/high. No empirical
data or model simulations were supplied to those design reviews. Independent
Claude review remains separately required.
