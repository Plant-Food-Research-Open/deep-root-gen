## Objective, baselines and ensemble policy

Freeze the observation operator exactly as [specified](OBSERVATION_CONTRACT.md).
Let `y_iv` be observed RLD and `p_sv(theta)` a generated seed-specific cube RLD
on training vine i's support. Let O/Z be occupied/digitized-zero cell sets.
Use the pooled positive **training-only** RLD 75th percentile as common scale
`a=max(q75,1e-6 cm/cm3)`, using NumPy `quantile(method="linear")`.
The scale has RLD units and normalization yields dimensionless loss; it is
**not measurement uncertainty**. Report both
scaled and physical-unit errors. Missing/empty O or Z is a contract error.

```text
ell(i,s) = .5 mean_O(abs(p-y)/a) + .5 mean_Z(abs(p-y)/a)
J_RLD(theta) = mean_i mean_s ell(i,s)
```

Average seed-level discrepancies, not just the discrepancy of an ensemble-mean
grid; otherwise incompatible random architectures can average into a good fit.
Generate three common seed architectures once per candidate and score each
against all six training supports. This is **shared-parameter average
reconstruction loss**, not six vine-specific parameter estimates or a proper
population-distribution score. Pairwise absolute error can favor a collapsed
ensemble near the median. Report dispersion as an adequacy diagnostic; do not
claim distributional recovery. A later population-distribution study needs a
proper distribution discrepancy and a separately accepted specification.
Do not select a best seed.

Compare a fixed literature-scale scenario (one candidate), RLD-only search
(25 candidates), and an architecture-constrained arm (24 candidates) **only if
the architecture observation definitions qualify**. For that arm, use
`J=.8*J_RLD+.2*J_A`, where `J_A` is equal-weight mean absolute error in
order-resolved organ counts and mean organ lengths, divided by training-only
SD (floors1 organ/1 cm), across orders1–5/vines/seeds. Empty-order mean length
is zero with a separate absent-order flag. Cross-vine SD is an ensemble scale,
not instrument error. No per-voxel order-length or total-length duplicate loss.

**Architecture gate:** archive labels are not consistently equal to XML nesting
depth. Keep labelled polyline counts/lengths as operational observations only;
establish their correspondence to generator organs before enabling `J_A`.
Insertion angles, branch spacing and topological attachment cannot be derived
from the current nesting uncritically. If this gate fails, leave that arm
`NOT_RUN`; do not reallocate its 24 candidates automatically. The RLD-only arm
still has value after synthetic qualification. No biomass arm is available.

A training-only separable depth×horizontal-radial density baseline is also
required. Its canonical lattice is x/y `[-200,200)` cm and depth `[0,250)` cm,
with10 cm cubes, and radial shells `[0,10),...,[280,290)` based on horizontal
cube-centre radius. These are fixed engineering bounds, not a complete
excavation volume. A target cube outside this lattice fails baseline support
and requires a new specification, never silent clipping or target-based tuning.

Let `Lbar` be the training mean soil length, `Lsum` its six-vine sum, `A_z`
training length assigned to depth-bin z, and `B_k` training length assigned to
horizontal radial shell k, using the prepared cube centres. Let `n_k` count
horizontal lattice cells in shell k; retain only shells with `n_k>0`, their
number `K`, and let `Z=25`. With dimensionless `epsilon=1e-6`, freeze

```text
D_z = (A_z/Lsum + epsilon)/(1 + Z*epsilon)
R_k = (B_k/Lsum + epsilon)/(1 + K*epsilon)
baseline_RLD(x,y,z) = Lbar*D_z*R_k/(n_k*1000 cm3)
```

Do not renormalize the baseline inside each target prism: sum predicted length
only over that support and report the remainder. It is a density baseline,
not a topology generator. Report occupied/zero MAE,
total length and overflow separately. These quantities derive from the target
and cannot establish independent architectural validity.

Training seeds4101–4103 and optimizer seed20261114 are fixed. A one-worker
deterministic candidate queue, stored before execution, replaces unrecorded
parallel scheduling. Reserve fresh seeds5101–5120, allocated 6/7/7 to
baseline/RLD-only/architecture arms; no later seed shopping. Choose the displayed
representative by median rank of generated valid-soil total length (seed breaks
ties), without the measured target. Retain all ensembles, not just that view.

For qualified unfitted morphology use attachment-angle and branch-spacing
distributions and full organ geometry, with dependencies disclosed. Until raw
digitizer topology is reconciled these observation-based endpoints are pending.
In the architecture arm, counts/mean lengths are fitted and cease to be
validation endpoints. RLD-derived rooting depth/spread/occupancy are diagnostics.

## Numeric acceptance and budget

All empirical thresholds below are **proposed engineering feasibility rules**,
fixed before fitting. No verified observation uncertainty supports a biological
PASS threshold; missing measurement error must not be invented.

| Gate/metric | Frozen proposed criterion | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Geometry/units | All common fixtures pass contract tolerances; no malformed graph | Algorithm qualification only |
| Reproducibility | Same seed/config gives identical nodes/edges in frozen runtime; resume signatures identical | Required reproducibility |
| Known synthetic truth | Truth discrepancy ≤1e-6; inverse search improves baseline and attains normalized voxel loss≤.01, total soil-length relative error≤1%, and exact organ counts | Noise-free matched-seed output recovery; parameter uniqueness not required |
| Shallow-band adequacy | Report observed length/RLD shallower than20 cm and the fixed translation's unavoidable error; no empirical launch while the incompatibility is unresolved | Required model-adequacy decision; do not optimize around it |
| Invalid geometry | No cycles/missing parents/nonfinite data; invalid length ≤1% of raw length | Engineering guard, not a biological threshold |
| Training density feasibility | J_RLD ≤.90×fixed-generator baseline and ≤training-only spatial baseline | Minimum practical improvement; report failure without retuning threshold |
| Reserved-vine density | Each arm's mean loss ≤1.0×spatial baseline; occupied and zero errors reported per vine | Descriptive assessment conditional on support, n=2 |
| Qualified unfitted traits | For each predeclared scalar trait, ensemble mean within 1 training SD of reserved mean, SD-ratio .25–4 | Exploratory adequacy; **UNAVAILABLE** if topology/definition gate fails |
| Bound saturation/overflow | Report all bound hits; exterior >20% triggers review, with no automatic exterior penalty | Unknown coverage prevents biological rejection based solely on overflow |
| Biomass/physiology | **NOT_EVALUATED** | No dry-mass join, diameter provenance or functional validation |
